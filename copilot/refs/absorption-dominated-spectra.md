Passive galaxies (typical of LRG targets) have spectra dominated by stellar absorption: the 4000 A break, Ca H and K, the G band and Mg b, with little or no emission. The current tool returns descriptive measurements and code-calculated alternatives, without a physical-confirmation verdict. It measures Ca H and K in absorption; other absorption features are not supported by this matcher. Missing emission, unsupported absorption, or missing wavelength coverage does not refute a redshift. A target-class range or neighbors from the same supervised encoder is not independent certification of an individual prediction. These local editorial notes provide context, not a replacement for model_z.
