The hydrogen Balmer series in the optical runs Halpha 6562.8, Hbeta 4861.3, Hgamma 4340.5, Hdelta 4101.7 A, with strength decreasing along the series. Because these rest wavelengths are fixed by atomic physics, multiple correctly identified Balmer lines can constrain a redshift hypothesis. Coincident peaks returned by this project's heuristic matcher do not establish those identities or eliminate degeneracy. In star-forming galaxies at z < 0.5, Halpha plus Hbeta is the most common such pair inside DESI coverage.
