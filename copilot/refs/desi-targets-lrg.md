LRG (Luminous Red Galaxy) targets are massive, passively evolving galaxies selected to lie at 0.4 < z < 1.1. Their spectra are dominated by absorption features - the 4000 A break with Ca H and K - and typically show weak or no emission lines. The current tool reports descriptive measurements, including Ca H and K absorption, with explicit support and modality limits. Missing emission is not evidence against the redshift. A target-class selection range is population context, not independent validation of an individual model_z or a reason to replace it. The tool does not emit a physical-confirmation verdict.
