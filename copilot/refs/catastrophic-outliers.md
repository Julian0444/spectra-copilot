In this project a catastrophic outlier is a prediction with |z_pred - z_true| / (1 + z_true) > 0.15, the eta metric reported for desi-fm. The dominant causes are line misidentification (the Halpha/[OII]/[OIII] single-line confusion), low signal-to-noise spectra, and sky-subtraction residuals mistaken for emission lines. The entropy concentration score can rank risk retrospectively. The historical 0.3 cut is exploratory, not a calibrated probability or validated service threshold. Line matching is heuristic; an apparent match does not physically confirm redshift.
