QSO (quasar) targets are broad-line AGN observed by DESI both as direct tracers (0.9 < z < 2.1) and as backlights for the Lyman-alpha forest (z > 2.1). Quasar spectra show broad emission lines (Lya, CIV, CIII], MgII) and reach the highest redshifts in the survey, z of 3.5 and beyond. Prior for verification: broad lines and a high z_pred point to a QSO. Model configuration (separate from the cited survey facts): v2.1 uses 100 bins in log(1+z) with configured endpoints z=0 and 6; bin centers are inside these endpoints. A maximum observed in a sample is not a configured model limit.
